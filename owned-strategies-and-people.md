## Strategies your books already support

1. **Beat the closing line** — best single skill metric (*Sharper*)
2. **Shop price / compare to sharp & juice-free lines** (*Sharper*)
3. **Estimate edge, then Kelly (or fractional Kelly) size** (*Sharper*, *Fortune’s Formula*, *Missing Billionaires*)
4. **Build ratings; adjust for injuries via player impact values; don’t equate rating gap to bet edge** (*Sharper*)
5. **Trade structure: alts, middles, scalps, buying/selling points** (*Sharper*)
6. **Baseball: project true talent, correct for cluster luck, bet vs Vegas divergence** (*Trading Bases*)
7. **Adaptive drift / shock modeling of sports prices** (*Mallios*)
8. **Quantitative / simulation culture over tip sheets** (*Perfect Bet*)
9. **Calibrated probabilities + bet-as-decision hygiene** (*Superforecasting*, *Thinking in Bets*)
10. **Use behavioral sports biases as model features** (*Scorecasting*)
11. **Only attack markets where skill can overcome variance + juice** (*Success Equation*)
